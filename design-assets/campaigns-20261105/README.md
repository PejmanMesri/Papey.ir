## Prompt set

Shared prompt (all three):

> Use case: ads-marketing / compositing. Create one premium ecommerce fashion hero photograph, extra-wide 3:1 landscape (around 2400x800). The reference is the actual garment sold by Papey: preserve its fabric color, cut and ALL printed artwork exactly; no invented print, no redraw, no model, no hands. Remove the original shop, mannequin stand and furniture. A floating/suspended garment with believable volume and subtle fabric folds, fully visible collar to hem and sleeves, occupies the LEFT 48% of the wide canvas, approximately centered at x=28%. Keep the RIGHT 48% clean dark charcoal negative space for real HTML Persian text to be placed later. Make it an art-directed cohesive luxury streetwear campaign, not a mock website. No text, no letters, no logos, no buttons anywhere in the background; the existing garment artwork is the only graphic. Garment must be large, crisp and realistically lit with soft shadows and textile detail. Background seamlessly falls to near-black #121212 at right. Avoid heavy fog, cheesy lightning, excessive objects, and cropped garment edges.

T-shirt variation:

> Campaign 1 / monochrome anime T-shirt: use the exact black oversized T-shirt BACK with its big white smiling anime smoke print. Graphite architectural studio, broad curved dark sculptural panel behind the shirt, controlled deep scarlet edge light on the far left, a very fine luminous red arc as one tasteful accent. High-end cinematic black and white with muted red. Preserve the entire white artwork on the garment.

Hoodie variation:

> Campaign 2 / hoodie collection: use the exact light heather-gray hoodie FRONT with the vertical orange anime half-face artwork, kangaroo pocket, hood and cuffs intact. Smoky warm graphite studio with a low matte warm stone plinth, a soft amber/copper circular light source behind the hoodie at left. Cozy editorial lighting but sharp fabric texture, restrained warm gray and orange. Entire hoodie fully visible.

Long sleeve variation:

> Campaign 3 / long-sleeve collection: use exact black crewneck long-sleeve FRONT, with its orange anime half-face chest print AND the original two printed sleeve details. Preserve all three printed designs and correct locations. Midnight charcoal studio with a subtle brushed metal plane and one diagonal muted ember-orange light accent behind the garment. Crisp modern technical streetwear editorial. Sleeves and cuffs fully visible, arranged naturally to show the sleeve prints.
